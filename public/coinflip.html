Why isn't it 50/50? Once you flip two heads in a row (HH), the very next tail finishes HHT — but HTH can never sneak in first from there, because any tail after those heads completes HHT. That asymmetry tilts the race: HHT wins with probability 2/3, HTH only 1/3. The expected number of flips to first see HTH is 10; for HHT it's 8.
